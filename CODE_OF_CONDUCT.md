# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or embodiment platform.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and open to technical disagreement.
- Give feedback about ideas and code, not people.
- Report results and limitations accurately, especially where safety or hardware claims are involved.
- Respect privacy and do not share another person's personal information without permission.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, intimidation, discriminatory language, personal attacks, deliberate misinformation about safety or performance, and publishing private information without permission are not acceptable.
